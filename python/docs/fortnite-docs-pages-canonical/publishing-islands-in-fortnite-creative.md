# Publishing Islands

Learn everything there is to know about publishing through the Creator Portal.

![Publishing Islands](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/37e33aa6-3983-48bb-8d0f-7954096d703f?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

Whether you’re publishing from Fortnite Creative or from Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN), the Creative Portal is where you go to publish your islands. The portal provides everything you’ll need to prepare and submit your island.

## The Publishing Workflow

When you're ready to reveal your island to the world, start the publishing process from Fortnite Creative or UEFN.

### Initiating the Publishing Process

From **Fortnite Creative** begin by:

- Clicking **Publish Island** in the Overview category of the Fortnite Creative **Island Settings**.

From **UEFN** begin by:

- Clicking the **Project** dropdown in the toolbar, then select **Publish Project**. This creates a new private version code.

### Publishing in Creator Portal

Once you’re in the [Creator Portal](https://create.fortnite.com/), you’re ready to create a new release and submit it for moderation.  To create a new release from your private version, follow the instructions below:

1. In the left navigation panel, click **Projects** to open the **Projects page**.

   [![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ed3e12ce-0bed-4f80-a32d-0e53fed2e801?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ed3e12ce-0bed-4f80-a32d-0e53fed2e801?resizing_type=fit)
2. Click on a project tile to open the island's **Publishing** page.

   [![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/533f7f09-feb6-4252-9f92-7096b43fd928?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/533f7f09-feb6-4252-9f92-7096b43fd928?resizing_type=fit)
3. Click the **Private Versions** tab. Select a private version in the list, and click **Create release**. A validity popup opens.

   [![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/5c73f5b5-1641-4530-ba97-848a8022eb39?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/5c73f5b5-1641-4530-ba97-848a8022eb39?resizing_type=fit)

   A validity popup opens, dismiss the window by clicking **Continue**. The publishing tabs open on **Game Details**.

   [![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ac33c49e-fe5d-4206-9c6a-4dc3150485d4?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ac33c49e-fe5d-4206-9c6a-4dc3150485d4?resizing_type=fit)
4. Fill out the island’s public metadata under **Game Details** and click **Next** to move to the **Rating** tab.

   [![Make sure to enter notes into the field under What has changed from the previous release](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/c1fc943a-64b5-4860-adea-105f80bf1790?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/c1fc943a-64b5-4860-adea-105f80bf1790?resizing_type=fit)

   Click to enlarge image.

   Island titles and thumbnails go through the Discover Filter before approval and advancing the publishing flow. For information on Discover Filtering and how it affects islands in Discover, see How Discover Works.
5. Provide an email address where you can receive ratings related communications from IARC, then click **Begin Questionnaire** at the bottom of the page. You receive a message that you are leaving the Creator Portal, and being redirected to the IARC questionnaire.

   [![Enter an email address for IARC to contact you.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f0474d2d-365e-4e5b-bb20-4cbda767cd9f?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f0474d2d-365e-4e5b-bb20-4cbda767cd9f?resizing_type=fit)

   Click to enlarge image.

   [![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/19a1bfd6-238e-42d1-9505-bc42725d5057?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/19a1bfd6-238e-42d1-9505-bc42725d5057?resizing_type=fit)

   - To add a second thumbnail to your promotional media, select **Create A/B Test**. To opt out of A/B testing, see [A/B Thumbnail Testing.](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/ab-thumbnail-testing-in-fortnite-creative)
   - To ensure you receive credit for your promotional assets, submit a release with those assets for content review and wait for approval before sharing them externally. This is best practice whenever assets are updated.
6. From the **Promotional Media** tab, upload your thumbnails, trailers, and lobby backgrounds. Then click **Next** to continue to the **Attribution** tab.

   [![Description: Upload the promotional material for your island.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/71e11aa3-7a75-4294-b47a-c70fd835c9b7?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/71e11aa3-7a75-4294-b47a-c70fd835c9b7?resizing_type=fit)

   Click to enlarge image.

   All islands are automatically entered into A/B thumbnail testing. To opt out of A/B testing, see the [A/B Thumbnail Testing document](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/ab-thumbnail-testing-in-fortnite-creative).
7. On the **Attributions** tab, select **Add New Attribution** to acknowledge any third-party assets used in your island. After entering all the necessary attributions, click **Next** to move to the **Visibility** tab.

   [![Add any attribution for third party assets you used on your island.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/49432e65-53a2-45eb-a379-9e903b41bef0?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/49432e65-53a2-45eb-a379-9e903b41bef0?resizing_type=fit)

   Click to enlarge image.
8. Under the **Visibility** tab, choose the **Project Visibility Status** for your island, and any additional publishing information.

   [![Set your island to Listed or Unlisted visibility, or choose Manually publish later.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ce8e5031-c55b-4f63-bac6-f62a7f21be32?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ce8e5031-c55b-4f63-bac6-f62a7f21be32?resizing_type=fit)

   Click to enlarge image.
9. Click **Submit for review** to send your release to moderation.

Your release enters a review process to ensure that the island adheres to Fortnite standards. The release you submitted is now listed on the **Releases** tab of the island's **Publishing** page.

If one of the publishing tabs is missing information, a red exclamation mark appears next to the tab.
