# Profile Pages

See your personal Profile Page in Discover!

![Profile Pages](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/df1f6cb6-844b-4ed0-b0b1-e1f8770df3ed?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

With **Profile Pages**, the Fortnite Discover UI makes it easier for players to find you and the islands you have published. Your Profile Page is where all your published islands are displayed.

## In-game Profile Page

When you become an active member of the [Fortnite Developer Program](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/island-creator-and-engagement-program-in-fortnite-creative), your own Profile Page is generated in Discover. Players visit Profile Pages by clicking the **MORE FROM** button on the bottom of the screen.

[![When players click the MORE FROM THIS CREATOR button they go to that creator page.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/3f830218-ed88-4d5a-8a60-ce98d523a046?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/3f830218-ed88-4d5a-8a60-ce98d523a046?resizing_type=fit)

Click to enlarge image.

As you publish islands through the Creator Portal, your Profile Page updates with the latest published islands

You’ll want to visit your Profile Page too — each island tile features a **concurrent user counter** that shows you how many players are playing your islands at that moment! Clicking an island tile takes you to the island's **Island Details page** where you can view information about the island.

[![An example of a Creator Page.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/2adafc60-9fc4-4c81-b2f0-66613cbba7e4?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/2adafc60-9fc4-4c81-b2f0-66613cbba7e4?resizing_type=fit)

If you decide you don’t want a particular island to be featured on your Profile Page, you can remove it. To do this, go to the Creator Portal and find the island you want to unlist. Below the island name, you will see a toggle button for **Listed/Unlisted**.

After toggling to **Unlisted**, your island will not appear in Discover or your Profile Page. You will need to do this for each of the published islands you want unlisted, as **Listed** is the default setting. Unlisted islands will not show up on your Profile Page.

### Developer Bio

The Developer Bio features your creator code, links to your social media profiles, a description of you as a developer, and the types of islands you create. You can create a custom header and profile image for your  page as well. To edit your bio description or social media links, or add a new header image or profile image, see **[Manage Your Profile Page](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/manage-your-creator-page-in-fortnite-creative)**.

### Developer Picks

Dislpays a list of islands by other developers that you like and play. To edit your picks, follow the instructions on **[Manage Your Profile Page](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/manage-your-creator-page-in-fortnite-creative)**.
