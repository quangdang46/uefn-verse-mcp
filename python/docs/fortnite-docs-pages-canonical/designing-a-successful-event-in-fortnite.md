# Designing a Successful Event

Learn the best practices for designing an event to self-promote your island.

![Designing a Successful Event](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/9f04a10c-8f1f-42f9-8750-11715f1baf53?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

In the fast-moving world of game development, the most successful islands are no longer just static maps. Recent experiences have proven that Admin Abuse events and live community spectacles can drive massive spikes in engagement, turning a standard game session into a viral moment.

To help you scale your experience, below is the blueprint for designing, marketing, and executing high-impact community events.

## Build the Hype

Never underestimate the power of anticipation. A successful event begins before the first player even spawns in. You want to create a temporal landmark, a specific moment in time that resets player motivation.

[![Use your community page to advertise and build anticipation for your event.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/98bb5be3-26bd-4037-9589-b3d9ee94cc1d?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/98bb5be3-26bd-4037-9589-b3d9ee94cc1d?resizing_type=fit)

Community Highlight

- **In-Game Countdown Timers**: Use the Real Time Clock or Timed Objective devices in UEFN to place massive, holographic timers in your lobby.
- **Discord Synchronization and Social Channels**: Match your in-game timer with a synchronized countdown in Discord and across your other social channels. Use Unix timestamps so every player sees the time remaining in their local time zone. Announce key milestones, teaser drops, sneak peek trailers, and schedule reminders across Discord and your external social feeds to reach players wherever they are engaged.
- **Fortnite Communities Integration**: Create and publish announcements in your Fortnite Community. Use this native ecosystem channel to share official countdowns, teaser reveals, and event details directly with players both on the web and inside Fortnite.
- **The "Coming Soon" Tease**: Don’t reveal everything at once. Use cryptic signage or silhouette teasers of new characters to spark theories on your community forums to generate buzz before the event even starts!

## Maximize Discovery

Use the tools in the Lobby. Your event shouldn't just be a secret for your hardcore fans; it should be visible to every player who clicks on your island.

[![Update your island thumbnail to advertise the status of your event.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f8a805d1-19cd-4ba9-abea-5bc6b1fe00af?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f8a805d1-19cd-4ba9-abea-5bc6b1fe00af?resizing_type=fit)

Island Thumbnail

- **Game Details and MOTD**: Community events are now more visible than ever. Your event details are displayed directly in the **Game Details** page and can appear in the **Lobby Message of the Day (MOTD)**. Use this space to clearly communicate when the admin will be live and what players can earn or experience.
- **Dynamic Thumbnails**: Update your island's thumbnail to reflect the event's state. Use a "Countdown" overlay for the days leading up, and switch to a bright "LIVE NOW" or "ADMIN IN-GAME" thumbnail during the actual event. This visual shift catches the eye in the Discovery tab and significantly boosts click-through rates.

## Admin Interaction and Spectacle

The "Admin Abuse" phenomenon works because it humanizes the creator. You act as a performer during the event and help to continue the hype even after players spawn into the experience. Think of the admin role like that of a "Dungeon Master" who can influence the world in real-time.

[![Create a Hall of Fame for your experience to reward top players.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/02d13ec3-ad09-4fc4-84ec-a3d4af5f5acb?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/02d13ec3-ad09-4fc4-84ec-a3d4af5f5acb?resizing_type=fit)

Hall of Fame

- **Live Interaction**: Use [Verse](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/verse-language-get-started-in-unreal-editor-for-fortnite) to trigger world-altering events—like gravity shifts, item "rain," or spawning massive bosses—that make players feel like they are part of a living movie.
- **The Power of Persona**: When entering as an Admin, wear a recognizable skin or use specific visual effects. Your goal isn't just to play; it's to be the entertainment. Whether you are helping players or playfully "trolling" them, you are the draw.
- **Community Highlights**: Dedicate a space in your map—a "Hall of Fame" or a "Community Corner"—to feature top players or funny clips from previous events. This rewards loyalty and makes players feel like part of the island's history.

## Capture, Share, Repeat

The event doesn't end when the server closes—create an echo effect that prepares players for the next planned event. The goal is to turn one event into marketing for the next one.

- **Create Shareable Moments**: Build in tension, surprise, or large-scale chaos that players will want to clip. Short, high-impact moments extend your event’s reach beyond the island itself.
- **The Recap Loop**: Encourage players to record their perspectives during the event.

  - Create a dedicated “Event Media” channel in your Discord or Fortnite Community where players can share clips.
  - Curate the strongest moments and edit them into short-form videos for platforms like TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

  Player-perspective highlights are one of the most effective tools for attracting new participants to future events.
- **Post-Event Discussions Within Your Community Spaces:** Whether on a forum, Discord server, or other owned channels. Prompt players with targeted questions such as:

  - Who should the Admin target next time?
  - What twist should happen in the next event?

  Structured follow-up discussions extend engagement beyond the live moment and give your community a sense of ownership over the evolving narrative.

## Be Innovative

Push the boundaries of what a "Live Event" can be in UEFN; use the tools to create unique, real-time social experiences that go beyond traditional gameplay.

Ready to take your event to the next level?

- **Experiment Boldly**: Use the latest UEFN tools to test new ways of interacting with your audience. Many high-performing islands rely on simple competitive loops, strong social dynamics, and high-stakes moments that are easy to understand and quick to join.
- **Design for Participation, not Spectation**: Avoid building events that feel like passive cutscenes. Give players influence through voting systems, shifting objectives, cooperative challenges, or admin-triggered twists that alter outcomes in real time.
- **Evolve the Forma**t: Treat each event as a learning opportunity. Analyze player behavior, retention spikes, and community feedback, then refine the format rather than repeating it unchanged.
