# Creating and Editing Campaigns

Create and edit campaigns to boost the visibility of your island and build brand awareness.

![Creating and Editing Campaigns](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/3f41083d-7304-46d3-8f07-86b040b5eed6?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

[Sponsored Row Campaigns](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#sponsored-row) help developers drive visibility for their islands in Discover. Sponsoring an island can help you reach a wider audience, grow your player base, and potentially increase your revenue.

[Campaigns](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#campaign) are [bid on in an auction](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/campaign-bidding-and-auction-mechanics-in-fortnite) based on the campaigns island owners create and manage through the Creator Portal.

To create a campaign:

- You must be enrolled in the **Fortnite Developer Program**.
- You must be the island owner with “Owner” permission to create a campaign.
- You must be **18+** years old to create campaigns.
- Your island must be published and listed.

## Create a Campaign

The Campaign page is a blank template for you to fill with the following information:

- **Campaign Name**
- **Select your Island**
- **Campaign Duration** (start/end dates)
- **Daily Budget**
- **Max Bid** ([CPM](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#cpm))
- **Payment Method**

Follow these steps to get started.

1. Name your campaign by clicking the **pencil icon** next to **New campaign**, then type a new name in the field.

   - The campaign name must be 3 - 100 alphanumeric characters.
   - The campaign name must be unique to your account.

   Once you have selected an island, the campaign name field uses the Island’s name by default, as well as the date you created the campaign.

   [![Click on the pencil icon next to New campaign and enter a new name in the field.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/93d10872-0a87-4001-bbad-640c3a930ad4?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/93d10872-0a87-4001-bbad-640c3a930ad4?resizing_type=fit)

2. Select one of your Island from the dropdown menu. A message appears informing you that your island content will be reviewed before it can be promoted.

   [![Select one of your islands from the Project Dropdown menu.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/511478c3-9978-4a43-9810-c66a63c4c3ca?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/511478c3-9978-4a43-9810-c66a63c4c3ca?resizing_type=fit)

3. Click the **Duration** field to open the calendar.

   - Inside the calendar, select **start and end dates** for your campaign by sliding the blue duration dots into place on the calendar.

Campaign end dates can be set up to 90 days from the start date. Click in the **Daily Budget** field to select your daily budget in United States dollars (USD). Keeping in mind the following:

Once data is available, the Market History chart shows the 7-day average Click Per 1,000 Impressions ([CPM](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#cpm)) by default (reviewable in 1 day, 7 day, 30 day, 90 day, Year To Date, and 1 year delineations).

## Editing, Pausing, and Canceling a Campaign

**Active (not delivering)**: The campaign is active but is not currently delivering impressions. For example, if a published release is not eligible for campaign delivery due to Ad Policy review, if a Creator unpauses the campaign, or if there is a payment issue that is unresolved.

Campaigns in this state will resume delivering once finalized.

Once you successfully submit a campaign you have the option to edit, pause, or cancel the campaign before it goes live. You can also pause or cancel your campaign at any time after the campaign goes live.

You can only edit the campaign name and duration once the campaign is live. If you want to change the bid or budget you will need to create a new campaign.

## Campaign Creation Guidelines

To create a successful campaign there are a few guidelines in place:

- During the campaign setup, only islands that are published, listed, and in good standing appear in the Island dropdown menu.
- Downranked islands do not appear in the Island dropdown menu.
- [Island versions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/campaign-faqs-in-fortnite#which-islands-are-eligible) that have been removed for Low Performance do not appear in the Island dropdown menu. To get a removed island to display in the dropdown:

  - Use your metrics in [Project Analytics](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/project-analytics-for-fortnite-games) to help evaluate your island's performance and determine the next steps.
  - [Update and republish](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/publishing-from-the-creator-portal-in-fortnite-creative) your island based on metrics learnings and create a new campaign.
- Campaigns cannot have the same name as any other campaign.
- Multiple campaigns cannot be scheduled for the same island for the same date
- Campaigns can only have a maximum duration of 30 days.
- The end of a campaign must be 90 days from the day you submit your campaign.
- If there is a payment issue for one of your campaigns, all active campaigns will be paused until you update your payment method and successfully pay for the outstanding balance; then the paused campaigns will resume.
- If you have an overdue charge on a campaign, all of your campaigns will be canceled after 7 days of non-payment.
- If you unpublish your island, active campaigns for that island will be paused because the island is no longer eligible to appear in Sponsored Row.

  Publishing a new island release does not automatically cancel campaigns. Campaigns continue delivering on the last approved release by default until the new release has been published. All new releases require additional moderation review once the release is published, which will automatically pause the campaign until the additional review has been completed. Campaign cancellation is reserved for explicit policy enforcement actions, not version iteration.

## Common Campaign Submission Errors

Islands must go through moderation before they are approved for Sponsored Row. In addition to the **[Fortnite Developer Rules](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-rules)**, [**Epic Games Content Guidelines**](https://legal.epicgames.com/en-US/epicgames/content-guidelines) and [**Epic Games Community Rules**](https://legal.epicgames.com/en-US/epicgames/community-rules) your content must also adhere to the Sponsored Row Content Rules (Section 5 of the Fortnite Developer Rules) to be eligible for Sponsored Row. Below is a list of the most common errors developers might make when creating a campaign. Islands must NOT contain the following:

- Real-world quick serve restaurants and real-world food and beverage.
- Cryptocurrency, non-fungible tokens (NFTs), or real world currencies and banks as part of their island experience.
- Political party affiliation for campaigning or messaging, including content related to religious or charitable causes, and Political Action Committees (PACs).

Sponsored Row islands must also adhere to the [thumbnail guidelines](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/thumbnail-image-policies).
