## Rules for Using TWDU Templates

In order to build with **The Walking Dead Universe** (TWDU) templates, there are rules you need to follow in addition to the [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-rules) and other existing agreements such as the Monetization Agreement.

### The Walking Dead Universe Brand Rules

When building using The Walking Dead Universe templates and promoting your islands that use TWDU templates you must adhere to the Fortnite Developer Rules, and all other relevant agreements and the following rules or you could risk experiencing the consequences discussed below.

The TWDU Brand Rules are enforced by Epic Games and Skybound. Skybound can report your island to Epic Games for violating the TWDU Brand Rules.

If your island is found to be violating the TWDU Brand Rules, your content will be removed, and you will not be eligible for engagement payout awards for that island. If you repeatedly violate the TWDU Brand Rules, you will lose access to the TWDU templates. Continued violation of these rules may lead to increasing restrictions or termination of your account.

### Never Use TWDU Assets With Third-Party IP

TWDU islands are already set up with the brand appropriate TWDU assets and assets you are allowed to use as part of the agreement as well as certain Epic Games owned or licensed assets. The IP Partner Licensing agreement makes clear that you cannot mix TWDU assets with other 3rd party Intellectual Property (IP), even if the owner of the IP has given their approval. You can import and use assets that you own all the rights to if they are compliant with the Fortnite Developer Rules, TWDU Brand Rules listed above and all other relevant agreements. You can also use other assets provided to you directly by Epic Games, excluding assets from another IP Partner Program partner (such as with LEGO® Islands).

If you violate this rule your island may not be eligible for engagement payout awards and may be removed from the ecosystem or disabled. Continued violation of this rule may lead to increasing restrictions or termination of your account.

## Monetizing TWDU Islands

Skybound is providing a limited license to their content for you to use their approved TWDU assets. This will allow you to bring an officially licensed TWDU experience into the Fortnite ecosystem, and create new types of islands for the Fortnite community. In return, you must comply with the terms of the IP Partner Licensing Agreement including the TWDU Brand Rules, and the Fortnite Developer Rules, Community Guidelines, and all other relevant agreements. The only approved TWDU assets and features are the ones accessed through the designated templates.

In consideration for Skybound’s license to you to use the TWDU assets to create your TWDU island, you agree to share 12% of your total island payout with Skybound. Epic Games will automatically deduct this amount from your engagement payout and distribute it to Skybound, and you will receive the remainder. You agree to this process when you agree to the IP Partner Licensing Agreement.

[In-island transactions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-in-fortnite) are currently not supported by this IP.

## Promoting your TWDU Island

When creating media for your island, reference the guidelines outlined in [Promoting Your Fortnite Island](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/promoting-your-fortnite-creative-island). Make sure images and videos accurately reflect the island or game mechanics you created and don’t copy another game or developer. Your promotion of your TWDU island must comply with the TWDU Brand Rules, [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-rules), Community Guidelines, and other relevant agreements (e.g., Section 3 of the Fortnite Developer Rules). The content and messaging in your promotion must be consistent with the content rules and guidelines in such rules and agreements and age appropriate for the target audience of your island.

### Your Island in Discover

In the Creator Portal, TWDU islands are automatically tagged with “The Walking Dead Universe” during the publishing process for a special tag that will appear in the product details page.

Developer-made TWDU islands will show up in Discover and are searchable, like any other Fortnite island.
