# Sword in the Stone Devices

Let a player claim the Infinity Blade to destroy enemies with a single blow!

![Sword in the Stone Devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/efde8eca-a964-4b2d-a42b-098141586b16?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

Use the **Sword in the Stone** device to [place](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#place) the [Infinity Blade](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#infinity-blade) on your island. When placed, the sword becomes available to any player regardless of team affiliation.

A player can use the Infinity Blade to destroy enemies, animals, spawned creatures, and structures around them in a single blow. With this weapon, the player becomes almost indestructible.

The device can be placed on floor pieces or on the ground. To make placement easier:

With these settings, the device will be much easier to position.

This device is great for a King of the Hill-style [game type](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#game-type) where there can only be one winner in the end.

This device cannot be customized.

## Removing the Device

You can remove this device by aiming at it with your phone and pressing **X**. Doing so will remove both the device and the foundation it’s attached to.

## Designer Tips

In [Play mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#play-mode), a player can claim the sword by pressing and holding **E** until the sword is released from the stone.

When using the Infinity Blade on vehicles, the sword deals 75 [damage](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#damage) until the last blow, where it deals 50 before blowing up both the car and the player holding the sword.

Players who use the Infinity Blade cannot hold other weapons while using this one.
