# Manage Your Profile Page
Manage your personal profile page to connect with players for your Fortnite Creative islands.
![Manage Your Profile Page](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/1e3d2f7c-46a7-4f99-aa89-a9b1a67b4906?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)
One of the options on the **Creator Portal** is the **Profile Page** , where you can introduce yourself to your players. Information you enter here shows up on your personal in-game Profile Page, and on the Fortnite.com developer page.
[![The Creator Page in the Creator Portal](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/4184113e-3354-43d5-8865-f6a4c9328d5c?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/4184113e-3354-43d5-8865-f6a4c9328d5c?resizing_type=fit)
You can use this profile to establish your personal brand, control which of your published games features on your personal Profile Page, and other information about you and your islands. You can even choose whether to share your personal play history.
Remember that **any of the info you add here will be publically available**!
##  Editing Your Bio
To begin editing your biographical details, follow the steps below.
[![](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f9e08b47-4197-4e0b-907e-45795f094258?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/f9e08b47-4197-4e0b-907e-45795f094258?resizing_type=fit)
_Click image to enlarge._
The steps below are for an individual developer account, as represented by a creator code.
  1. Select **Profile Page** from the menu.
  2. Write a brief bio (up to 300 words) about yourself and/or your games. This is your pitch, so make it count!
  3. Upload a [Profile Image](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/manage-your-creator-page-in-fortnite-creative). This is the image that displays in the top right corner of your page. Dimensions for this image are 192x192.
  4. Upload a **Cover image**. This image displays at the top of your Fortnite page. Dimensions for this image are 1400x480.
You can download a template for your Profile Image and your Cover Image by clicking the **link** under the **Upload now** button.
The Creator Portal needs a default profile image for Profile Pages. Upload a customized image asset as part of editing your Profile Page. The image can be your company logo or even the Fortnite character you use as your main avatar.
All images go through moderation. While the profile image is in moderation the page displays a blank or default image.
Until a decision has been reached, you cannot upload a new profile image.
  5. Add any social links you want to direct players to. This includes:
     * X (Twitter)
     * Instagram
     * Discord
     * TikTok
     * YouTube
Select a social platform, then add your handle.
  6. Click the **Save** button to save all your changes, then submit.

All profiles go through a moderation review process before being published. During the review, all fields are updated with an **In Review** status and you cannot make changes to your profile.
[![During the review process all fields have an In Review status added.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/af2f6211-fbbe-4ac0-b69c-cfa32e8b8efa?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/af2f6211-fbbe-4ac0-b69c-cfa32e8b8efa?resizing_type=fit)
_Click image to enlarge._
Once approved, you receive an email when your Profile Page is published live to Discover. If your Profile Page fails the review process, you will also receive an email.
You can edit your profile details at any time after it’s approved, but doing so sends your profile through moderation again.
[![Once approved, you receive an email and your Creator Page is published live to Discover.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ee7ec582-31fb-4473-b6f7-b49f77498fb6?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ee7ec582-31fb-4473-b6f7-b49f77498fb6?resizing_type=fit)

###  Featuring Your Islands
Decide which of your islands to feature on your Profile Page by choosing the order the islands appear.
To do this:
  1. Open the [**Creator Portal**](https://create.fortnite.com/welcome) and navigate to your island’s **Publishing page**.
[![Open the Creator Portal and navigate to your island’s Project page.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/544ad053-ad49-4783-8c59-4655684cfcad?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/544ad053-ad49-4783-8c59-4655684cfcad?resizing_type=fit)
  2. From the project's navigation menu toggle the island to either **Listed** or **Unlisted**.
Toggling the island to **Listed** means your island appears in Discover or your Profile Page.
To unlist an island, toggle the island to **Unlisted**. Your island will not appear in Discover or on your Profile Page.
[![From the project's navigation menu, toggle the island to either Listed or Unlisted.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/58285491-8711-419f-8814-d24e75ce8edd?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/58285491-8711-419f-8814-d24e75ce8edd?resizing_type=fit)
_Click image to enlarge._
  3. To Feature an island, click the **Star icon** next to the island name. Listed islands tagged with the Star icon display in the **My island display order** list.
[![Click the Star icon next to the island name.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ee65ca0d-04e2-4772-bb95-4c0bf98c8b8c?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/ee65ca0d-04e2-4772-bb95-4c0bf98c8b8c?resizing_type=fit)
_Click image to enalrge._
Featured islands display in a **Featured** section of the Profile Page as shown in the image below:
[![Featured islands display in a Featured section.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/66cf0711-0c87-4aba-8997-98e342144532?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/66cf0711-0c87-4aba-8997-98e342144532?resizing_type=fit)
  4. Change the island order by clicking the **arrow icons** at the end of the island row to move them up and down in the order.
[![Change the islands’ order by clicking the arrows at the end of the island row.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/49c57a30-a4f3-4b66-97f5-01189f56af5b?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/49c57a30-a4f3-4b66-97f5-01189f56af5b?resizing_type=fit)
_Click image to enlarge._

Do this for each of the published islands you want unlisted, as "Listed" is the default setting. Unlisted islands will not show up on your Profile Page.
###  Favorite Developer Count
As players favorite you, a tally of your total favorites is recorded in the Creator Portal. On your Profile Page in the Creator Portal, you can see two types of favorite analytics:
  * **Total Favorites** - The total number of favorites you currently have.
  * **New Favorites per Day/Week/Month** - The number of new favorites you gain according to day, week, and month.
[![View the Favorite analytics by visiting the Profile page in the Creator Portal and clicking on the ellipsis menu at the end of the Favorites displayed.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/72f7c315-3f32-48df-836b-4af985894d0c?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/72f7c315-3f32-48df-836b-4af985894d0c?resizing_type=fit)

To view these analytics, visit the Profile page in the Creator Portal and click the ellipsis menu at the end of the Favorites displayed. To download your Favorites analytics, click **Download**.
##  Editing Developer Picks
Developer Picks provides a way for you to promote other developers and islands you play or are inspired by in a special row called **Developer Picks**. You can display up to ten islands from other developers.
You cannot accept money from another developer to advertise their islands in your Developer Picks row. Please note that at this time, paid promotions and collaborations are prohibited in Developer Picks.
You can pin and order other developers' islands by doing the following:
  1. Click **Confirm** to agree that you won’t take money to advertise another island developer.
[![Click Confirm to agree that you won’t take money to advertise another island creator.](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/c8b2bcdc-cb3a-42bf-beda-174105419c91?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/c8b2bcdc-cb3a-42bf-beda-174105419c91?resizing_type=fit)
