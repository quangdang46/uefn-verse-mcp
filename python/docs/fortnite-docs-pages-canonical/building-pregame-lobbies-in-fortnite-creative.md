# Building Pre-Game Lobbies

Use this tutorial to build your own Pre-Game Lobby.

![Building Pre-Game Lobbies](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/e64efa66-30a7-4441-a903-a04c290f17ad?resizing_type=fill&width=1920&height=335)

The **pre-game lobby** is where players initially spawn as they wait to join games. Use pre-game lobbies as a dedicated space for players to learn game rules while [matchmaking](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary).

Design lobbies to communicate game rules by using informational devices like [**Billboard**](using-billboard-devices-in-fortnite-creative) and [Pop-Up Dialog](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-popup-dialog-devices-in-fortnite-creative).

## Building Your Lobby

Browse the [Prefab](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) and [Gallery](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) tabs and consider a [theme](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) for your pre-game lobby.

Use items from the **PreFab** tab if you want to use a pre-built structure, complete with props and decorations.

Use items from the **Gallery** tab if you want to build a unique pre-game lobby, putting pieces together on your own. Challenge yourself by mixing pieces from different galleries.

Build structures that are at least two tiles high, with a floor and a roof.

For a faster [build](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#build), press **B** to open the **Options** panel ([Quick Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#quick-menu)). Locate and set **Building to Prop** to **Off**.

### Designer Tips

[![Pinbrawl’s Pre-Game Lobby Overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/a20cbc64-4641-419f-b534-629d0fb91d76?resizing_type=fit)](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/a20cbc64-4641-419f-b534-629d0fb91d76?resizing_type=fit)

Offer players a fun environment while they wait. As shown in the photo above, this lobby offers a winding bookcase that players can hop on.

Once the pre-game lobby structure is complete, begin adding devices like [Player Spawn Pads](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-player-spawn-pad-devices-in-fortnite-creative).

Check out [**Building Arenas**](building-arenas-in-fortnite-creative) to create structures for players to battle, like in [Pinbrawl](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/pinbrawl-island-tutorial-in-fortnite-creative).
